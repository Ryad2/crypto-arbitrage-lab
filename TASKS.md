# Initial task board

Use this board until the team agrees how to track work. Keep one owner per task, assigned only after they accept. Move substantial work into a GitHub issue and link it here.

| ID | Priority | Task | Owner | Status | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SETUP | Now | Prepare shared brief and repository | Ryad | Complete | Project documentation and private remote are available |
| TEAM | First | Confirm team and arrange kickoff | Ryad | Open | Roles, GitHub invitations, availability and contribution boundaries are agreed |
| ROUTE | First | Select one initial route | Unassigned | Open | Pair, venues/chain, hypothesis, access and exclusions are recorded |
| DATA | Next | Capture a synchronized sample | Unassigned | Blocked by ROUTE | Source and local timestamps, provenance and quality checks are documented |
| COST | Next | Specify executable-edge accounting | Unassigned | Blocked by ROUTE | Depth, fees, latency, funding/borrow and failure/inventory costs are explicit where relevant |
| REPLAY | Next | Build a reproducible first replay | Unassigned | Blocked by DATA and COST | A held-out run, baseline and adverse assumptions can be reproduced |
| SHADOW | Later | Observe live without trading funds | Unassigned | Blocked by REPLAY | Opportunity decay, latency and execution assumptions are logged |
| REVIEW | Later | Present findings and decide next step | Team once formed | Blocked by SHADOW | Evidence and negative cases are reviewed; any pilot is separately approved |

No real-time performance, profitability or launch date is promised by this plan. The team will set dates after confirming availability and route access.
